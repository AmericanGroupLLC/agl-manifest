# The same app has two different names on the two stores

Found 2026-09-30 by comparing each app's App Store name with the Play
listing title actually shipped in its repo. 11 of 43 disagree.

| App | App Store | Google Play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BuddyPlay | iBuddyPlay | BuddyPlay |
| CarDeal | iCarDeal | CarDeal |
| EventSpark | iEventSpark | EventSpark |
| HomeWise | iHomeWise | HomeWise |
| MileMaster | iMileMaster | MileMaster |
| MyCard | iMyCard | MyCard |
| MyFinance | iMyFinance | MyFinance |
| NewsBoard | iNewsBoard | NewsBoard |
| RoomCraft | iRoomCraft | RoomCraft |
| Uscis-Case | USCIS Case Lookup | Case Tracker for USCIS |
| yourtube | YourTube: Watch & Discover | yourtube |

## The `i` prefix

Nine of the eleven are one pattern: an `i` prepended on Apple only —
iBuddyPlay, iCarDeal, iEventSpark, iHomeWise, iMileMaster, iMyCard,
iMyFinance, iNewsBoard, iRoomCraft. It is in this repository's own store
copy, so it is deliberate rather than something Apple did.

iMaps and iMeasure are not in that list and should not be: those names
genuinely begin with `i` on both stores.

Two reasons to look at it rather than leave it:

* The same product is findable under two names. Search, reviews, word of
  mouth and any link the support site publishes all split in half.
* An `i` prefix reads as an Apple product. Apple's own guidance is against
  names that suggest an association with Apple, and this account is already
  being reviewed under Guideline 2.1 for having no review history.

## The other two

* **Uscis-Case** — "USCIS Case Lookup" on Apple, "Case Tracker for USCIS"
  on Play. Both are reasonable; they are just different.
* **yourtube** — "YourTube: Watch & Discover" on Apple, `yourtube` on Play.
  The Play title is the repository name in lower case and reads as a
  placeholder rather than a decision.

## Not changed

Renaming a store listing is a branding decision and eight of the nine
`i`-prefixed apps are In Review right now, so a rename would disturb a
review in flight. Recorded here for a decision rather than acted on.
