---
name: approval-workflow
description: "Prepare a founder approval request for anything on the CHARTER's human-decision list. Use whenever work reaches a decision the office may not take."
---

Write `reports/approvals/<date>-<slug>.md` with:
- **Decision needed** — one sentence.
- **Why now** and what it unblocks.
- **Exact action** the founder takes (link, button, command) — or what the office
  will do once approved.
- **Evidence** — checks run and their output.
- **Risks / how to undo.**
Then post it as an `ai-office` issue labelled `needs-founder` and stop. Do not
proceed until the founder answers in the issue or in chat.
