---
name: company-planning
description: "Turn a founder goal into a project with tasks, owners, acceptance criteria and approval points. Use when the founder states a new goal."
---

1. Create `projects/<slug>/plan.md`: goal, why, acceptance criteria (testable),
   tasks (owner role, depends on, evidence required), approval points (from
   CHARTER), risks.
2. Check the goal against company/policies.md (no fake data, store rules,
   production safety). Flag conflicts before planning around them.
3. Open one `ai-office` issue per task; link them from the plan.
4. Record the decision to start in company/decision-log.md.
