---
name: weekly-review
description: "Weekly review — what shipped, what is stuck, which automations failed, what the founder still owes. Use every Monday."
---

1. Read the week's `reports/*-standup.md`, closed and open `ai-office` issues,
   and `gh run list` for every automation.
2. Write `reports/<date>-weekly.md`: shipped (with evidence), stuck (why, owner),
   automation reliability (runs, failures, fixes), founder decisions outstanding
   (MA items, approvals), next week's top 5.
3. Update projects/*/plan.md status and company/decision-log.md.
