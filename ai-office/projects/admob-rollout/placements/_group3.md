## Findings across the group

1. **Interstitial preload no app asked for.** `AglAds.initialize()` always preloads an interstitial (`packages/agl_ads/lib/src/ads.dart:94`). So apps that never call `onTransition` (daily-pulse, NearServe, PlantPulse v2, and GroceryMatch once its call is removed) still send one unused interstitial request every launch. agl_ads needs an opt-out flag. That is a package change, so it needs its own approval.
2. **agl_ads lock is too old for the new formats.** Every agl_ads app in the group pins a resolved-ref older than cb21ee5 (BuddyPlay 97e9a7f, daily-pulse/GroceryMatch d5e5b72, NearServe/PlantPulse 7b3c98e, yourtube f0fc3b2). Run `flutter pub upgrade agl_ads` before wiring native. The CI defines for native and rewarded already exist: `scripts/agl_paths.py:142-159` passes every format in docs/admob-ids.json.
3. **Possible wrong age-rating answer (not verified against ASC).** BuddyPlay, daily-pulse, GroceryMatch, NearServe and PlantPulse ship ads but have no `store/ratings/<repo>.json`, so they fall back to `default.json:16 "advertising": false`. Apps with ads that do have their own file say true.
4. **Rewarded: none in this group.** No app here has a real free quota or premium extra that could be given away without gating a core feature. PlantPulse and BuddyPlay are also excluded by product and audience rules.
5. **apps.json `admob` block is stale for TripNest and yourtube.** It shows Google test ids, but the apps ship the real AGL ids.
