## Proposed replacement for section 1 to 7 (sections 8 and 9 stay)

**Last updated:** set to the publication date.

**1. What we collect and why**

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Email address, or your Apple or Google sign-in identifier | Create and secure your account | Our backend (Supabase). With Apple's "Hide my email", we receive only the relay address |
| Phone number, only if you choose to confirm one where the app offers it | Show "phone confirmed" on your Passport | Our authentication provider |
| Display name, date of birth, gender, what you're looking for, interests, prompt answers | Your profile, shown to the members you are shown to. Your date of birth keeps DriftDate 18+; others see your age, not your birthday, in the app | Our backend |
| Photos you upload | Your profile | A private storage bucket. Only members who can see your profile can open them, through short-lived links |
| Your area: a 3-digit ZIP prefix, county and state | Place you in a ring (ZIP area, county, state, everywhere) | Worked out on your phone. Your exact position is never stored or sent. Other members see only "Same ZIP area", "Same county" or "Same state", never a distance |
| Waves, matches, unmatches and messages | Matching and chat | Our backend. Only the two people in a chat can read it. After an unmatch, neither of you can read it any more; it is kept for our safety team in case of a report and deleted when either account is deleted |
| Blocks and reports you file, including the reported message's text or the reported photo | Keep you safe and let our safety team act | Our backend. Reports are read only by our moderators, never by the person reported |
| That you accepted our Terms of Use, with the version and the time | Required before you can use DriftDate | Our backend and your device |
| Crash reports, diagnostics and app usage events | Fix crashes and keep the app working | Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics, Performance Monitoring). They contain no messages, photos or profile text |
| Device advertising identifier and ad interactions | Show and measure ads | Google AdMob. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked first. Change your choice in Settings → Privacy choices |

**2. What stays on your device and is never collected**
- Safe Meet plans, your trusted contact and check-ins.
- Your exact location. The county lookup runs on your phone from a bundled Census table.
- Your chat drafts, your passes, and which ring you last looked at.
- Your contacts. DriftDate never reads your address book.

**3. Safety and moderation**
Everyone agrees to our Terms of Use before using DriftDate, and they include zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive members. You can report a profile, a photo or a single message, block anyone, or unmatch at any time. A person on our safety team reviews every report within 24 hours. We may remove content, hide a profile while we review it, or ban an account, which also stops it from signing in. A profile reported by several members is hidden automatically until a moderator reviews it. We keep a record of each moderation decision, and we may share information with law enforcement where the law requires it or someone's safety is at risk.

**4. Who we share data with**
We do not sell your personal information. We use these service providers: Supabase (accounts, database and photo storage), Google Firebase (crash reports and diagnostics), Google AdMob (ads), and, once enabled, an email provider that delivers new-report alerts to our safety inbox. Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them. Other members see your profile, your photos and your Passport facts (email confirmed, phone confirmed, Apple or Google sign-in, month you joined), but never your email address, phone number or exact birthday.

**5. Children**
DriftDate is strictly for adults 18 and over, in the United States. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18. Profiles with a date of birth under 18 are refused, and accounts found to belong to minors are removed.

**6. Deleting your account and your rights**
Delete your account in Settings → Delete account. This removes your account, profile, photos (including the files), prompts, waves, matches, messages, blocks and your terms record. Reports you filed about someone else are kept without your name, so our safety team can still act on them, and our log of moderation decisions keeps an account id. You can also email contact@americangroup.ai from the address you signed up with, and we act within 30 days.

**7. Retention and security**
Your data is kept while your account exists, and deleted as above when you delete it. A chat ended by an unmatch is kept, unreadable by either person, until either account is deleted. All traffic uses HTTPS, and photos are in a private bucket.
